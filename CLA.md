## How to sign

You do not need to send anything by email. When you open your first pull
request, an automated check will ask you to confirm this Agreement by posting
the following comment on the pull request:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your GitHub username and the signing commit are recorded in
[`signatures.json`](https://github.com/youngminnnn/wooi/blob/cla-signatures/signatures.json)
on the `cla-signatures` branch. Signing once covers all of your future
contributions to the Project.

If you are contributing on behalf of a company, contact
<youngmin3306@gmail.com> for a Corporate CLA before submitting.
